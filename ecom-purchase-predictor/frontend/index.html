About the Models

Three scikit-learn classifiers trained on e-commerce customer behaviour data.

🌲

Random Forest

An ensemble of decision trees that votes on the final outcome. Robust to noise and typically the most accurate of the three.

RandomForestClassifier
🌿

Decision Tree

A single interpretable tree that splits on feature thresholds. Fast and transparent — you can trace exactly why it made a call.

DecisionTreeClassifier
📈

Logistic Regression

A linear model that estimates the probability of purchase. Great baseline; excels when the decision boundary is roughly linear.

LogisticRegression
